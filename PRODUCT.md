# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Three distinct roles with different contexts and devices:

- **Admin** — manages operations: schedules bookings, assigns washers, tracks payments, reviews analytics. Works on desktop. Needs dense, scannable information without cognitive overload.
- **Clients** — book and track car wash appointments. Uses both mobile and desktop. Needs a guided, low-friction booking flow and a clear view of upcoming/past jobs.
- **Washers** — receive and complete assigned jobs. Primarily on mobile. Needs quick access to today's queue and job details; no clutter.

## Product Purpose

WashFlow is a full-stack car wash management platform. It replaces phone/paper scheduling with a structured booking workflow: clients pick a service and slot, admin assigns a washer, washers log completion, admin tracks revenue and staff.

Success looks like: a small car wash operation running its full day from one tool, with no spreadsheets and no missed bookings.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, precise, unhurried. The system equivalent of a professional detailer — competent and quiet. Not flashy, not sterile.

Three-word personality: **clear, capable, trustworthy**

## Anti-references

- **Generic Tailwind UI kit clones** — no off-the-shelf shadcn/Flowbite look; avoid stock component aesthetics where every input, badge, and card looks lifted from a template.
- **Over-engineered enterprise SaaS** — no Salesforce-style nav depth, no modal stacking, no six-column data grids by default.
- **Cheap service-industry apps** — no clip-art iconography, neon accent colors, busy gradients, or stock-photo-heavy marketing patterns bled into the app shell.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over cleverness** — every screen has one primary action; the interface never competes with the task.
2. **Role-appropriate density** — admin screens can hold more data; client and washer screens stay guided and light.
3. **Earned trust** — visual quality signals operational reliability; no decorative elements that don't carry information.
4. **Mobile-aware by default** — washer flows must work on a phone in a parking lot; client booking must feel natural on mobile even if admin is desktop-first.
5. **Neutral with a point of view** — modern SaaS aesthetic that recedes behind the work, but has considered opinions on spacing, type weight, and color — not a kit clone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Sufficient contrast on all interactive elements, keyboard navigable, screen reader friendly. Consider reduced-motion preferences for any animation added later.
